# Teach me Daddy — product context

## Register

**Product.** This is an authenticated tool someone opens five times a day for
five months. Design serves the task; it is not the product.

The one exception is the account gate, the only surface a newcomer sees before
committing. It carries the personality. Everything behind it stays quiet.

## Users

The owner and a handful of friends. Adults, French-speaking, learning Hebrew
seriously against the 5-month plan in `plan-hebreu-5-mois.md`: 4 hours a day,
5,000–6,000 words, roughly 95% coverage of everyday Hebrew.

Not beginners browsing an app store. People who have already committed and need
an instrument that keeps up.

## The scene

A learner drilling Hebrew on a phone in short bursts: in bed at night, on a bus,
between meetings. Five to eight sessions a day, every day, for five months. Often
one-handed. Often tired.

That scene forces dark, but a **quiet** dark: low emission, high text contrast,
nothing glowing. Long sessions punish decoration. It also forces density and
thumb-reachable controls, and it forbids anything that makes a 30-second session
feel like a production.

## Product purpose

Replace Anki and a paper plan with one instrument:

1. Learn the alphabet, print and cursive, including the look-alikes.
2. Drill vocabulary on a real SM-2 scheduler.
3. Run the 5-month plan: daily blocks, weekly focus, metrics, coverage curve.

## Personality

Dry, competent, quietly funny. The name and the rotating taglines carry the
joke; the study surfaces do not repeat it. Humour appears at the edges only:
the gate, empty states, the end of a session. Never mid-drill.

The tone of the writing is direct and a little blunt. It states what happened
and what it costs. It never congratulates you for showing up.

## Anti-references

- **Generic dark SaaS.** Glass cards, blurred colour fog, gradient text, glow
  accents, muted grey-on-grey. This is what the app looked like before and it is
  the single thing to design away from.
- **Gamified language apps.** Mascots, confetti, streak flames, cartoon rounding.
  The plan's own rule is "zero missed days" — that pressure is real, and it
  should read as a fact, not a party.
- **Corporate courseware.** Stock photography, safe blue, cheerful padding.

## Strategic principles

1. **Hebrew is the ornament.** The letterforms are the only decoration this app
   needs. Everything else is quiet so the glyphs carry the screen.
2. **Density over air.** A tool for people mid-task shows more, not less. Wide
   empty pill cards were hiding how little was on screen.
3. **Accent means action.** The teal marks the primary action, the current
   selection, and progress. Nothing else. If it decorates, it is wrong.
4. **A session is 30 seconds.** No page-load choreography, no staggered
   entrances. The screen is ready when it appears.
5. **State is legible without colour.** Mastery, due, lapsed, mature all read
   from text and position first; colour only reinforces.

## Accessibility

Text on the base surface holds at least 7:1. Never white on the accent: the
teal is bright, so filled accent surfaces take near-black ink. Touch targets are
44px minimum. Pinch-zoom must stay enabled. Motion respects
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
